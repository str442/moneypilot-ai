# MoneyPilot AI

MoneyPilot AI is an AI-powered personal finance manager designed for students and young adults.

The goal of this project is to help users track income, expenses and savings goals, while providing useful insights about their financial habits.

## Problem

Many students and young adults know how much money they receive every month, but often do not know exactly where their money goes, how much they can save, or how long it will take to reach a financial goal.

MoneyPilot AI aims to solve this problem by combining expense tracking, financial dashboards, savings goals, scenario simulation and AI-generated insights.
